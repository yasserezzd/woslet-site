What Woslet does

Woslet lets you browse local stores, place delivery orders with them and talk to them about your order. Each store sells and delivers its own products: the store sets its menu, prices, delivery fees, delivery area and opening hours, and is responsible for the products it prepares and delivers.

Your account

  • Give accurate details, especially your phone number and delivery location, so the store can reach you.
  • Keep your sign-in private. You are responsible for orders placed from your account.
  • You can delete your account at any time from the app.

Orders and payment

  • An order is a request to the store. It is confirmed when the store accepts it; the store may decline it.
  • You pay the store directly, in cash on delivery or by Whish. Woslet does not take card payments.
  • You can cancel an order while the store has not yet accepted it. After that, contact the store through the order chat.
  • Delivery times shown in the app are estimates.
